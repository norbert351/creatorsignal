HOW IT WORKS

From noise to your next video

Four stages, running on their own. You just approve what sounds right.

01

Listen

Connect your YouTube channel, TikTok videos, X mentions and Telegram group. The Mind pulls every new comment, continuously.

02

Understand

Comments are classified into signals — questions, requests, praise, critique — and grouped into topics by meaning, not keywords.

03

Remember

Your superfans are tracked by engagement. The Mind remembers who asks, what they care about, and what you've already covered.

04

Recommend

Repeated unanswered questions become opportunity cards with demand scores, plus a daily digest of what to make next.

NOT A CHATBOT

An agent that works on its own

CreatorSignal isn't a chat window bolted onto your analytics. It's a persistent agent with memory that completes a job: turning audience noise into content decisions.

  • ✓Detects repeated unanswered questions across platforms
  • ✓Ranks opportunities by real demand, not guesswork
  • ✓Pushes a daily digest of what to make next
  • ✓Drafts replies to your superfans in your tone
  • ✓Remembers what you've covered, so it never repeats
