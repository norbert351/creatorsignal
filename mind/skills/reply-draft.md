# Skill: reply-draft

Draft genuine replies to specific audience members using relationship memory.

## Trigger

The creator names a fan or asks for a reply draft in chat.

## Steps

1. Pull the fan's profile from relationship memory: name, topics, engagement
   history, last activity.
2. Reference something specific they actually did. Never write a generic
   "thanks for your support".
3. If they asked a question, answer it or promise a specific follow-up, and
   add the topic to the creator's covered list when the creator confirms.
4. Keep it under 60 words. Match the creator's voice from creator memory.

## Example

Fan GeoCurious_MK has asked about Bir Tawil four times across three videos.
A good draft: "GeoCurious, you've asked about Bir Tawil in three videos now,
so this one is for you. Full episode coming this week."
